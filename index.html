MacMCMD

MacMCMD is for editing settings on provisioned computers with personalization settings blocked. It requires only an unelevated terminal and can set accent colors, dark mode, wallpapers (default wallpapers and custom ones), and most other dock/appearance settings.

In other words, it lets you personalize school macs.
